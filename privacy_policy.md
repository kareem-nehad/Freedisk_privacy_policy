# Freedisk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Freedisk ("the app", "we") helps you find duplicate photos, duplicate audio and junk files on your Android device andsee where your storage is going. This policy explains what the app accesses and what is shared.

## What the app accesses on your device

* **Photos, videos and audio** (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`; on Android 12 `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):to read file sizes, dates and contents so duplicates can be detected and shown to you. This analysis happensentirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded, copied off the device, or shared with us or anyone else.
* **Usage access** (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`, optional): only to read how much storage each installed app uses. It isgranted by you in system settings and the app works without it, with estimated app sizes.
* **Installed apps list**: used only to display app names and storage sizes in the Storage screen.
* **Deleting files**: files are removed only after your explicit confirmation. Photos and audio are moved to thesystem trash, where Android keeps them for about 30 days. Junk (cache, temporary files) is deleted after youconfirm.

## What we collect

We do not have accounts, servers or a database, and we do not collect, store or sell personal information.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and use, according to[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its[partner-sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites):

* device identifiers such as the **Advertising ID**,
* IP address and approximate location derived from it,
* device and app information, and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks), to deliver and measure adsand prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, the app asks for your consentthrough Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time from**Storage > Ad privacy settings**. You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings(Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Children

Freedisk is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Data retention and deletion

The app keeps no personal data. App preferences (such as your language) are stored only on your device and are removedwhen you uninstall the app.
